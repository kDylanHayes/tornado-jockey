# Roadmap

1. **Skeleton** (done): target hash, build generator, objdiff config, docs, CI.
2. **Analysis baseline** (done): scripted Ghidra analysis and delinking, region map in [regions.md](regions.md), first target object (zlib `adler32`) delinked and loaded in objdiff.
3. **First matching unit** (done): zlib 1.2.2 `adler32.c` compiled with cl 13.10.3077 `/O1` matches 100% in objdiff, proving the delink → compile → diff loop.
4. **Libraries** (in progress): build ODE, zlib, libjpeg 6b and libpng from upstream source with matching flags, and mark those units complete. FreeImage's zlib 1.2.2 (8 units), libpng 1.2.8 (14) and libjpeg 6b (37) are done at 100%, and so are FreeImage 3.7.0's own C++ code (21 units, 0x559e00–0x56717b) and ODE 0.5 with OPCODE (49 units, 0x586fd0–0x5bda8f). The libpng 1.0.5 and zlib copies in block 1 belong to the prebuilt D3DX8 library and are linked from `d3dx8.lib`.
5. **Progress reporting** (done): `tools/report.py` reports progress over all of `.text`, the measured report, a progress page and the badge data are committed in `progress/`. A decomp.dev listing is a sign-in away; see [maintainers.md](maintainers.md).
6. **Engine and game code** (started): decompile Tongas2 and game units. Matched so far, all 100%: `APPOPTIONS` (display and sound settings in optionswtj.dat, 10 functions), `SRESULT` (script result values, 19 functions), `SYSMONITOR` (heap and CPU usage sampling, 5 functions), `TOKENS` (the script tokenizer, 7 functions), `FILELOADER` (plain file reading, 15 functions), `FILELOADERWKZ` (reads `name.wkz` instead when it exists, 15 functions) and `WKZ` (the zlib-compressed .wkz format, 6 functions). `RESOURCES` (the resource system: its 26 type managers, the master and copy indexes, groups, channels and volume settings, 93 functions) also matches 100% of its code. `RESOURCE` (the base classes of what the resource system holds: `RESOURCEMASTER`, which hands out copies of a loaded resource, `RESOURCE`, one copy, and `OBJECT`'s placement code, which follows a parent actor or a tag and works out where to draw the object) matches 35 of its 36 functions; `RESOURCEMASTER::TrimCopies` is still off. `DECAL` (textured quads in the world or on screen: `DECALMASTER`'s shader, animation and rotation properties, the decal manager, and each decal's mesh copy, frame animation and camera facing) matches 38 of its 45 functions; `DECAL::Update` and `DECAL::Start` are the furthest off. `PATH` (paths of up to 100 points joined by straight lines, Catmull-Rom or Hermite curves, with an up vector, radius, angle and script command per point, and the editor commands that insert and delete points) matches 42 of its 56 functions; the curve sampling and nearest-point searches are the furthest off. `MATHS` (the `V3` vector helpers: box and segment tests, Catmull-Rom splines, triangle normals and headings) is in with 4 of its 6 functions at 100%; the two spline functions are each a couple of instructions off. `SOUND` (sound types, with their volume, distance fade and fade out when let go, the sounds playing from them, and the exclusive groups only one sound of which plays at a time, kept in a `std::map`) matches 100%. `UNICODESTRING` (the pool of 0x200-byte pages wide strings live in, with its defragmenter, and the "WC" encoding that stores wide text as three characters each) matches 12 of its 14 functions; `UNICODESTRING_MANAGER::Defrag` is down to block order and `UNICODESTRING::ToAnsi` to one load. `GLOBALDEFS` (named values from GLOBALDEFS.txt, each held as a string, 14 functions) and `ACTORRESOURCES` (up to 16 resources an actor template carries, each with a position, angles, scale, opacity, sort order and bone, 21 functions) match 100%. `LIGHT` (light types' colors, range and attenuation, the lights placed from them and the per-group light counts the renderer plugin's manager keeps, 22 functions) matches 21 of them; `LIGHTMASTER`'s constructor zeroes its colors with different registers. `PATHREPOSITORY` (loads PATHREPOSITORY.txt's named paths into path resources and keeps their points in a growable array of vectors, 30 functions) matches 29 of them; `PATHREPOSITORYMASTER::SetFileName` is at 92%, with different registers and a smaller stack frame. `GUIGENERIC` (GUI element templates with up to 32 resources each, with their screen position and depth, angles, spin, scale, opacity, sort order and bone, 23 functions) matches 100%. `GUIGROUP` (GUI screens and panels: their activate, step and mouse commands, mouse area, sounds and up to 32 controls with position, size, visibility and a behavior such as fade or blink, 15 functions; the setters are inline and land in `GUISLIDER`) matches 100%. `GUISLIDER` (a GUI group with a slider visual and a command run when it moves, 40 functions counting the `GUIGROUP` setters) matches 100%. `GUICHECKBOX` (a GUI group with commands run when checked and unchecked, 13 functions) and `GUICHECKGROUP` (a group of check boxes with how many may be checked at once, 12 functions) match 100%. `GUIVISUAL` (up to 32 textures or screen decals a GUI element draws, with placement and the progress bars they can show, 29 functions) matches 100%. `GUITEXT` (up to 32 lines of GUI text with fonts, placement, alignment, colors and click commands, 14 functions; most setters are inline and land in `GUITEXTEDIT`) and `GUITEXTEDIT` (GUI text the player types into, with caret, length limit, allowed characters and an Enter command, 37 functions counting the `GUITEXT` setters) match 100%. Their text is held in `CWSTRING`, the wide twin of `CSTRING`. `SCREENDECAL` (pictures under data/art/ drawn flat on the screen, with a blend mode and scroll speed, and the copies placed in screen coordinates, 23 functions) matches 22 of them; the copy's constructor stores its screen-placement defaults in a different order. `BONEDMODEL` (boned model types under data/art/ with their render passes and normal normalizing, and `BONEDMODELRESOURCE`, the base of the placed models, which plays the animation it was given when it starts, 25 functions) matches 100%. `FX` (effects from FX.txt that start up to 32 resources one after another, each after its wait and for its life, placed relative to the effect, attached to a bone of another of its resources or kept at a point on the screen, 41 functions) matches 39 of them; `FX::Spawn` and `FX::Update` are each a few instruction orders off. `CURVE` (the base of the game's curves: `CURVEMASTER`'s width, texture, end colors and opacity and camera facing, and each curve's strip of quads through up to 129 points, drawn with a copy of a mesh made for it) matches 19 of its 24 functions; `CURVE::UpdateMesh`, which works out the strip's corners, is the furthest off at 75%. `POLYTRAIL` (trails from POLYTRAIL.txt, with a life, a rate of points dropped a second and a velocity the points drift with; each trail drops points behind the object, scales and moves them with it, fades the old ones and fades out entirely when let go, 25 functions) matches 24 of them; `POLYTRAIL::Stop` inlines `CURVE::Stop` where the original calls it. `SHELL` (opening a page in the program .htm files open with, starting a process, and opening a file through the shell, 4 functions) matches 100%. `EMITTER` (particle effects: `EMITTERMASTER`'s birth, mid-life and death ranges, emitter shapes and picture-driven emission, the emitter that spawns and kills particles, and each particle's placement, forces, path following and fading) matches 78 of its 91 functions; the particle constructor and `PARTICLE::Update` are the furthest off. WildTangent's leaderboard SDK, which the game links in, is also matched: `WTLEADERBOARD` (4 functions), `WTLEADERBOARDIMPL` (builds the submit and view URLs, 3 functions plus a static string) and `WTCRYPT` (the score obfuscation, 3 functions).
7. **Full link**: link all objects with link 7.10.3077 and reproduce `Tornado.exe` byte-for-byte.
