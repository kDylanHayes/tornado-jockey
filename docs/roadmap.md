# Roadmap

1. **Skeleton** (done): target hash, build generator, objdiff config, docs, CI.
2. **Analysis baseline** (done): scripted Ghidra analysis and delinking, region map in [regions.md](regions.md), first target object (zlib `adler32`) delinked and loaded in objdiff.
3. **First matching unit** (done): zlib 1.2.2 `adler32.c` compiled with cl 13.10.3077 `/O1` matches 100% in objdiff, proving the delink → compile → diff loop.
4. **Libraries** (in progress): build ODE, zlib, libjpeg 6b and libpng from upstream source with matching flags, and mark those units complete. FreeImage's zlib 1.2.2 (8 units), libpng 1.2.8 (14) and libjpeg 6b (37) are done at 100%, and so are FreeImage 3.7.0's own C++ code (21 units, 0x559e00–0x56717b) and ODE 0.5 with OPCODE (49 units, 0x586fd0–0x5bda8f). The libpng 1.0.5 and zlib copies in block 1 belong to the prebuilt D3DX8 library and are linked from `d3dx8.lib`.
5. **Progress reporting** (done): `tools/report.py` reports progress over all of `.text`, the measured report, a progress page and the badge data are committed in `progress/`. A decomp.dev listing is a sign-in away; see [maintainers.md](maintainers.md).
6. **Engine and game code** (started): decompile Tongas2 and game units. Matched so far, all 100%: `APPOPTIONS` (display and sound settings in optionswtj.dat, 10 functions), `SRESULT` (script result values, 19 functions), `SYSMONITOR` (heap and CPU usage sampling, 5 functions), `TOKENS` (the script tokenizer, 7 functions), `FILELOADER` (plain file reading, 15 functions), `FILELOADERWKZ` (reads `name.wkz` instead when it exists, 15 functions) and `WKZ` (the zlib-compressed .wkz format, 6 functions). `RESOURCES` (the resource system: its 26 type managers, the master and copy indexes, groups, channels and volume settings, 93 functions) also matches 100% of its code. `RESOURCE` (the base classes of what the resource system holds: `RESOURCEMASTER`, which hands out copies of a loaded resource, `RESOURCE`, one copy, and `OBJECT`'s placement code, which follows a parent actor or a tag and works out where to draw the object) matches 35 of its 36 functions; `RESOURCEMASTER::TrimCopies` is still off. `DECAL` (textured quads in the world or on screen: `DECALMASTER`'s shader, animation and rotation properties, the decal manager, and each decal's mesh copy, frame animation and camera facing) matches 38 of its 45 functions; `DECAL::Update` and `DECAL::Start` are the furthest off. `PATH` (paths of up to 100 points joined by straight lines, Catmull-Rom or Hermite curves, with an up vector, radius, angle and script command per point, and the editor commands that insert and delete points) matches 42 of its 56 functions; the curve sampling and nearest-point searches are the furthest off. `MATHS` (the `V3` vector helpers: box and segment tests, Catmull-Rom splines, triangle normals and headings) is in with 4 of its 6 functions at 100%; the two spline functions are each a couple of instructions off. `SOUND` (sound types, with their volume, distance fade and fade out when let go, the sounds playing from them, and the exclusive groups only one sound of which plays at a time, kept in a `std::map`) matches 100%. `UNICODESTRING` (the pool of 0x200-byte pages wide strings live in, with its defragmenter, and the "WC" encoding that stores wide text as three characters each) matches 12 of its 14 functions; `UNICODESTRING_MANAGER::Defrag` is down to block order and `UNICODESTRING::ToAnsi` to one load. `SHELL` (opening a page in the program .htm files open with, starting a process, and opening a file through the shell, 4 functions) matches 100%. `EMITTER` (particle effects: `EMITTERMASTER`'s birth, mid-life and death ranges, emitter shapes and picture-driven emission, the emitter that spawns and kills particles, and each particle's placement, forces, path following and fading) matches 78 of its 91 functions; the particle constructor and `PARTICLE::Update` are the furthest off. WildTangent's leaderboard SDK, which the game links in, is also matched: `WTLEADERBOARD` (4 functions), `WTLEADERBOARDIMPL` (builds the submit and view URLs, 3 functions plus a static string) and `WTCRYPT` (the score obfuscation, 3 functions).
7. **Full link**: link all objects with link 7.10.3077 and reproduce `Tornado.exe` byte-for-byte.
